<objective>
Ship the frame-perimeter check end-to-end as a thin vertical slice: floor-project the
world-space bounding-box corners of `FRAME_`-tagged parts, take the 2D convex hull
(d3-polygon), compare its perimeter length against the R101/R104 season limit, and
render the hull as an inline SVG so the team sees exactly what footprint was measured.

Purpose: This is the highest-complexity check in v1 and the taut-string-wrap definition
the rulebook actually uses (GEOM-01). It reuses Phase 2's enrichment-in-route pattern
(fetch geometry in the route, merge onto `Fact[]`, keep the check pure/sync) and the
tri-state Verdict/UNKNOWN gating so a dropped part never produces a false PASS (D-02
"prefer false FAIL"). The SVG render (GEOM-02, D-07) makes the measured geometry
visible and discloses the configuration.
Output: Two pure geometry utils, the perimeter CheckFn, the route's 5c per-part
enrichment block, and the HullRender panel component.
</objective>

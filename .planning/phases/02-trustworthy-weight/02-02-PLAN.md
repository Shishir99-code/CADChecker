<objective>
Extend the shared facts + verdict shapes and ship the material-default audit end-to-end so a user can click "Check Now" and see the exact set of weight-contributing parts Onshape would otherwise silently omit from the mass total.

This is the phase's breaking-change slice: `Fact` gains `massKg`/`materialAssigned`; `flatten-assembly.ts` surfaces the assembly `parts[]` join list (with cross-document version fields) and a `groupPartsByElement` helper; `Verdict` becomes tri-state (PASS/FAIL/UNKNOWN) with an optional dual `measured:{lb,kg}`, `affectedParts`, `caveats`, and a single documented `KG_TO_LB` constant (D-02, D-06, D-11). Every Phase-1 consumer of the old `Verdict` (`occurrence-count.check.ts`, `frame-tag-presence.check.ts`, the panel's mirrored `CheckReportVerdict`, and `ReportTable.tsx`) is migrated in lock-step so the build stays green. The route (`check.routes.ts`) performs the per-Part-Studio mass + material fetch/merge between fact-gather and engine-run — checks stay pure and synchronous. The merge selects the Onshape address per group (`w/{workspaceId}` for parts in the assembly's own document, `v/{documentVersion}`/`m/{documentMicroversion}` for referenced documents) and degrades gracefully: a referenced-document group the token cannot read (HTTP 403) is skipped and its parts left UNRESOLVED (`massKg`/`materialAssigned` = `undefined`) rather than counted as 0 kg — the silent-undercount this tool exists to prevent (02-MASS-PROPERTIES-CONTRACT.md F2/F3). A new `material-audit.check.ts` (WGHT-01) lists offending parts and is registered as a first-class report row.

Purpose: Success Criterion 1 — the material audit is visible and trustworthy. It is the gate the two weight verdicts (shipped in 02-03) will depend on.
Output: Extended `Fact`/`Verdict` contracts, the enrichment merge step with per-group cross-document addressing and graceful per-group degradation, and the shipped material-audit check rendered in the panel.
</objective>
